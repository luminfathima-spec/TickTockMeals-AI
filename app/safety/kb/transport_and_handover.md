# Safe Transport, Transit Windows, and Volunteer Verification

Surplus food redistribution is time-critical. The distribution chain from donor kitchen to recipient table must minimize transit duration and ensure unbroken protection against temperature abuse.

Requirements for courier volunteers and handover:
- Handover Inspection Gate: Before accepting custody of surplus meals intended for human recipients, the volunteer must conduct sensory verification: confirm absence of sour/off odors, verify normal appearance, confirm container cleanliness and lid integrity, and check temperature.
- Temperature Verification: Food labeled as hot-held should still be visibly steaming or hot to the touch (ideally >= 60°C). Food labeled as refrigerated must be chilled to the touch (<= 8°C).
- Transit Time Envelope: Volunteer transit time plus delivery buffer must fit comfortably inside the food's safe window. If travel time exceeds the remaining safe window minus safety margin, the trip is non-viable and must be re-routed or downgraded.
- Rejection on Disqualification: If food fails sensory inspection at donor collection, the volunteer must not load it for human consumption; it must immediately trigger cascade reclassification.
