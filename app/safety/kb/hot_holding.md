# Hot Holding Requirements for Cooked Food

Cooked surplus food maintained in active hot-holding equipment (such as bain-maries, commercial warmers, or insulated steam tables) must be kept continuously at or above 60°C (140°F) throughout the holding duration.

Key parameters for hot-held surplus food:
- Internal food core temperature must be periodically validated with a sanitized food probe thermometer.
- Food continuously held at >= 60°C may be safely distributed for up to 6 hours from completion of cooking, provided the containers remain covered and protected from environmental aerosols.
- If holding temperature drops below 60°C, the food enters the danger zone. It must be rapidly reheated to 74°C within 1 hour or consumed within 2 hours.
- Food displaying dried crusts, severe thermal scorching, or steam-vent condensation pooling should be inspected visually before authorization for human distribution.
