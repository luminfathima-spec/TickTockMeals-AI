# Cross-Contamination Prevention and Transport Containers

The transfer of physical, chemical, or biological hazards from raw food, hands, surfaces, or soiled containers to cooked surplus food represents one of the leading causes of foodborne outbreaks during mass feeding.

Transport container and hygiene mandates:
- Food contact surfaces must be food-grade stainless steel or BPA-free food-grade plastic, washed and sanitized prior to receiving surplus food.
- Open, unlidded containers exposed to outdoor airborne dust, vehicle fumes, or insect contact during staging are prohibited; food must be covered or sealed before courier pickup.
- Strict physical separation between cooked ready-to-eat dishes and raw meats or unwashed vegetables during transit.
- Containers showing oil stains on outer walls, chemical residue, or broken rims must be rejected by the courier.
