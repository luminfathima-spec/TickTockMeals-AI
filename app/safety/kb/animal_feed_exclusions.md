# Animal Feed Safety and Toxic Ingredient Exclusions

When surplus food passes beyond safe human consumption limits, it can be redirected to registered animal rescue shelters. However, human surplus meals frequently contain ingredients that are toxic or fatal to animals (especially canines and felines).

Strict exclusions from animal feed rescue:
- Allium Species (Onion, Garlic, Leeks, Chives): Contain thiosulfates and disulfides that induce irreversible hemolytic anemia and Heinz body formation in dogs and cats. Gravies, sambars, and biryanis rich in onion and garlic must NEVER be routed to animal shelters.
- Theobromine and Caffeine (Chocolate, Cocoa, Tea/Coffee): Toxic cardiotoxic alkaloid causing fatal arrhythmias in canines.
- Xylitol and Concentrated Sweets: Xylitol triggers acute liver necrosis and severe hypoglycemia in dogs. Sugary Indian sweets (mysore pak, gulab jamun, jalebi) cause acute pancreatitis and osmotic diarrhea.
- Excessively Salty, Oily, or Spicy Dishes: Cause hypernatremia and acute gastrointestinal mucosal inflammation.
- Eligible animal feed items: Plain cooked white rice, plain chapati, boiled potatoes, plain cooked lentils (without onion/garlic seasoning), and fresh vegetable trimmings.
