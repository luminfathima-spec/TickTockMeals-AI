# Packaged Food Integrity and Expiry Standards

Packaged and shelf-stable foods (biscuits, packaged bread, tetra-pack milk, canned foods, snacks) carry defined manufacturer expiry timelines that supersede standard cooked food danger-zone calculations.

Guidelines for packaged surplus:
- "Use-By" or "Expiry" Date: Food past its printed Use-By date cannot legally or ethically be distributed for human consumption under FSSAI regulations.
- "Best-Before" Date: Best-before reflects peak organoleptic quality; non-perishable dry goods up to 15 days past best-before may be donated only after physical packaging integrity check and sensory confirmation.
- Packaging Integrity: Ripped seals, blown/swollen cans, vacuum loss, punctured tetra packs, or rodent gnawing instantly disqualify the package and mandate discard.
- Unlabeled sealed foods without printed batch code or expiry date require human volunteer inspection before release.
