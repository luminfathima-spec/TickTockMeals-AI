# Dairy, Milk, Paneer, and Curd Safety

Dairy products, fermented milk dishes, curd rice, paneer gravies, and milk sweets are high-risk perishable items due to neutral or slightly acidic pH, rich nutrient content, and high water activity supporting rapid pathogen growth (Listeria monocytogenes, Staphylococcus aureus, lactic spoilage).

Critical dairy safety boundaries:
- Ambient storage of curd, raita, or curd rice in tropical temperatures must not exceed 2.5 to 3 hours. Ambient exposure beyond 3 hours causes rapid lactic acid souring, whey separation, and potential toxin accumulation.
- Any visible surface discoloration, yellowish patches, or mold spores on dairy or curd rice forces immediate classification as HIGH risk and exclusion from human meals.
- Unpasteurized milk products or raw milk preparations are excluded from surplus donation programs.
- Refrigerated storage at <= 4°C extends safe life for sealed paneer or pasteurized curd to 18 to 24 hours.
