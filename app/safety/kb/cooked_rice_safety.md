# Cooked Rice and Cereal Safety Limits

Cooked rice dishes (plain steamed rice, biryani, fried rice, pulao, khichdi) carry a distinct microbial hazard from Bacillus cereus. Bacillus cereus produces heat-resistant endospores that survive standard boiling and cooking temperatures.

Critical safety rules for cooked rice:
- When cooked rice is held at ambient room temperature, surviving Bacillus cereus spores germinate, multiplying and producing heat-stable emetic toxins (cereulide). Emetic toxins are not destroyed by reheating or re-frying.
- Under ambient room storage, cooked rice must never exceed 3.5 to 4 hours from the time of cooking. Any ambient rice older than 4 hours represents high microbiological risk and must not be consumed by humans.
- When hot-held continuously at >= 60°C, spore germination is suppressed, allowing a safe window of up to 5 hours.
- When refrigerated at <= 5°C within 2 hours of cooking, rice remains safe for up to 24 hours.
- Visual inspection: sticky slime, foul or sour odor, or discoloration in cooked rice indicates advanced bacterial proliferation.
